| Offline case | Expected observation | First discrepancy to investigate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Straight forward, then pure left | All four wheel-velocity vectors agree and rotate a quarter turn between requests | Encoder sign, module installation offset, field transform |
| Positive pure yaw | Each vector is tangent to a circle around the chosen center. A wheel at (+0.3,+0.3) with ω=1 has velocity (−0.3,+0.3) m/s | Swapped wheel locations, yaw sign, wrong origin |
| Measured 179°, desired −179° | Short wrapped motion of about +2°. Equivalent speed reversal is valid when paired with the opposite wheel direction | Angle wrapping, feedback direction, degrees versus radians |
| Wheel held still while azimuth turns in replay | Compare indicated drive motion with the module's documented coupling convention | Double correction or wrong ratio/sign |
| Wheel slip or delayed camera | Log gyro angle, wheel distances and azimuths, timestamps, estimates, camera transform and uncertainty separately | Odometry drift, stale measurement or incorrect camera frame |
