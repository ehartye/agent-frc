# Review the selected robot for fabrication and publication

Use the [agent placement guard](../../docs/checked-placement.md) when moving or installing components. Then repeat the complete cold census and the operating sweep on the issued assembly.

Compare every physical occurrence in the cold successor with the [pre-CAD target roster](../../docs/article-functions.md). Reconcile added, missing and substituted parts, then qualify actual retention and clearance across the complete assembly.

Check saved installation features against independently frozen world datums, including fixed hardware. Trace each loaded mount through its qualified structural members to the chassis. Identify cosmetic covers and inspect the direction each actual joint can carry. Commission the physical donor, recipient, finite article and supported acceptance interval for every loaded duty. Verify measured separation through a free release, including a held or reversed drive as a control. Review registered numerical disagreements between mating solids using independent interior witnesses and complementary regions. [Operating criteria](../../docs/operating-intent.md)

Start with the exact archive and pose the student would receive. Name the scope, planned operations and candidate revision. Freeze the required operating travel, handoffs and known defects from the commissioning plan. On cold reopen, compare them with the signed rooted Fusion joint inventory, including fixed and hidden assemblies. A usable engineering model can stay in the project while any required check remains open.

1. Cold-reopen the Fusion archive. Reconcile occurrence hierarchy, installed transforms, native body bounds and exact placed STEP solids against the expected build. Bind a purchased article to the official part, configuration, model bytes and item-level rights. Bind a shop-made article to its editable native design, drawing, stock grade and revision. Identify the repeat count and installed position of each identical fastener. If shape comparisons disagree, [check their shared interior and retain the raw measurements](../../docs/mechanical-methods.md#resolve-a-contradictory-source-shape-measurement).
2. Measure the complete placed assembly. Inspect every separate group and every pair with common material. Follow an explicitly retained connection to the chassis from every part. For an intentional press fit, threaded engagement or moving clearance, identify both bodies, the retaining feature, permissible gap/intersection, tolerance and inspection. Resolve any invalid intersection against the same two native bodies at the reviewed pose. For [disagreeing repeated interfaces](../../docs/mechanical-methods.md#compare-repeated-installed-interfaces), inspect each original pair and compare its native result in the same installed pose. Retest the whole travel or prove a conservative bound between poses.
3. Ask the shop to reconstruct the hardest joint using its actual tools. Check datums, tool radius, approach/withdrawal, tolerance accumulation, fastener grip, joint restraint, weld access and material/temper where applicable. Replay assembly, inspection and removal within the installed robot. [Shop capability](../../docs/fabrication-capabilities.md) · [Fabrication chain](../../docs/fabrication-chain.md) Commission each installed [blind or through screw stack](../../docs/fastener-stacks.md), its bearing seat, full thread engagement and actual bottom clearance. Check whether a fastened stack can pull through its structural mount. Move the whole stack in the [retention and service test](../../docs/mechanical-methods.md#can-the-fastened-assembly-pull-through-its-mount) and verify the stop and its load capacity.
4. Trace each governing load through the real supports. Test the adverse direction, extension, asymmetric loading, jam or power loss where applicable, expected impact, repeated duty and service state. Compare stress and stiffness to source-qualified limits. Preserve unknown fatigue life, material properties and physical test status. Challenge support assumptions and reproduce the numerical result. [Mechanism hardening](../../docs/mechanism-hardening.md) · [Coupled support](../../docs/coupled-support.md)
5. Run the [mechanical-readiness review](../../docs/mechanical-readiness.md) on the candidate. Check independently that the duty roster covers the installed design. A new showcase model needs a passing review, including shop and structural duties, and verified rights for every published derivative. Preserve the previously released model if the candidate has a failed or open check. Record the exact failing pair or duty and the next discriminating test in the project.

For every defect found by an external study, reproduce its detection using the installed plugin, retain the failing control and rerun the actual candidate. [Method coverage and project issue register](../../docs/mechanical-methods.md)

If repeated clashes, inaccessible attachments or an unattainable scoring role survive local fixes, revisit the premise with `frc-whole-robot-design`. Compare the inherited mechanism with a different architecture under the same role, space, supply, timing and evidence before choosing the next iteration.

Use the [Fusion authority and revision handoff](../../docs/fusion-authority.md) to establish the one current saved native design, the STEP exported from that design and the identity of the engineering evidence. Recheck the register when resuming in another checkout or before replacing a published view.

For a mechanism that transfers a game piece, [sweep the actual released object through the installed frame](../../docs/loaded-transfer.md), including its arrival and a jam/reset case. Compare the current vendor purchase BOM to the model’s as-drawn revision for every supplied assembly.

Follow the underlying observations for each qualified joint through to their governing case and numerical acceptance limit. For a replacement showcase verify the rendered pixels and each playable native joint trajectory, including compound motion. [Publishing the checked geometry](../../docs/mesh-correspondence.md)

Verify mounted hardware at alternate native poses and match each installed constituent's physical material and density to its drawing or supplier requirement. Compare unchanged bodies to their previous native issue. Record motion limits and source-backed material substitutions before issuing a replacement. [Installed hardware and stock](../../docs/mechanical-methods.md)

For mechanically linked drives, command only the independent native axes and read back the followers. Compare signed travel, installed phase, obstruction and recovery against the commissioned operating interval. [Review coupled operating motion](../../docs/operating-intent.md)

For a loaded duty, check the entire rotating article and the simultaneous forces and moments on each body through the required interval. Compare the actual nested supplier solids against their commissioned mounting sites. [Finite motion, support and placement](../../docs/operating-intent.md)

For a loaded transfer, require its independently registered trajectory law and all synchronized measurements. Reconcile one-sided article momentum across every custody transition and any independently observed impact. Check the full dwell and retain a matched within-boundary and crossed-boundary case. [Commission measured motion](../../docs/loaded-transfer.md#register-the-executed-trajectory)

Before promoting a model, run the [local source and final-site release verification](../../docs/private-model-release.md#local-release-command). Keep its receipt with the project and retain the approved public version while any required qualification is open.

For a shared-station or deferred native readback, [stage its exact plugin and project dependencies](../../docs/native-job.md) before dispatch. Keep the same source issue through export, each cold inspection and release.

For a numbered design, commission the whole-robot or subsystem publication scope, then bind both delivery trees to the selected current native issue. [Active model selection](../../docs/private-model-release.md#keep-a-retired-robot-online-during-a-numbered-restart)

For stalled oblique solid pairs, [measure a finite directional clearance bound](../../docs/mechanical-readiness.md#separate-two-oblique-solids-before-a-costly-intersection) on the exact installed articles and keep checks of contact, load and motion in their original scope.
