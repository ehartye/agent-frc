# Which role changes the alliance result?

Read the release-day rules artifact and its field profile. Separate ordinary points, terminal capacity, ranking incentives, penalties and legality. Leave an incentive unevaluated until its project-owned rules and witness exist. Count reachable pieces by period, scoring station and return time. Subtract travel, queues and time reserved for terminal actions from the available handling time.

Give students a card for each plausible role. Record what it does for each partner, what it consumes or blocks, the minimum repeatable rate, field access, build and driver burden, fallback and the observation that would overturn the premise. Include a simple independent baseline and a complementary role. Evaluate how often a partner could use a delivery and who actually gets the scoring credit.

Run the [matched alliance study](../../docs/season-meta-lab.md) before selecting a mechanism. Keep the other robots fixed within each comparison. Change partners, opponent routes, scarcity and terminal occupancy between contexts. Inspect individual waits and failures even when scores tie. Save a held-out setup before using results to choose or revise a role. List the worst observed margin, opportunity cost and the partner mix at which the choice changes.

Hand the selected role, scope, demand window, unresolved rule questions and fallback to the strategic narrative. A modeled score establishes a field opportunity. A competitive mechanism rate needs accepted events from the installed design.
