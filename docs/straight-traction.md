# Straight traction and encoder/ground separation: development scope

This original bounded longitudinal model replaces the no-slip drivetrain equations for its explicitly straight-only scenarios. It retains the actual compiled dual-encoder/PWM controller, common electrical supply and actual CAD-derived auxiliary wheel load. It is not MapleSim, MuJoCo or a calibrated tire model. The former planar/neutral tests remain required regressions; their turning coverage does not transfer to this contact plant.

The opt-in pipeline requires3 native extraction tests,3 existing IO tests,13 prior coupled tests and8 traction tests, no failures/skips. Missing native geometry, source/profile mismatch, unsupported charging/asymmetric commands or numerical domain violations fail. Exact controller bytes and native properties are consumed, not hash labels alone. Ordinary Node/Java test commands do not execute this native pipeline.

## Explicit hypothesis, not configured chassis evidence

The prior45kg chassis/radius/gearing/supply assumptions remain. Per-side rotational inertia0.02kg m2 and coefficient0.8 are new unvalidated assumptions with unknown uncertainty, not properties extracted from the configured chassis ledger. The real bare wheel remains an auxiliary component, not a substitute drive tire. Vehicle mass includes translating components; side inertia represents only rotation at the wheel coordinate, with no extra translational contribution. Normal load is assumed exactly half of mg on each of TWO effective side contacts on a flat level surface. This is not a six-wheel load-distribution, drop-center, suspension,3D CoM or tipping model. Friction coefficients0 and0.05 and a high-grip mathematical case are explicit test hypotheses, not measured carpet values. The zero-friction controller launch test is0.1s; extending that chosen trajectory to0.5s invokes unsupported regenerative behavior and must reject. The1s low-traction and nominal launch scenarios are separately declared, not an arbitrary-duration driving guarantee. No manufacturer durometer/material is converted into a friction coefficient.

## Independently reviewed equations and numerical limitations

The [Stewart/Trinkle2000 primary paper](https://www.cse.lehigh.edu/~trink/Papers/STicra00.pdf) motivates impulse-momentum contact stepping and bounded Coulomb friction/maximal work. The following is an original restricted two-contact derivation, not an implementation or validation of the paper's full method.

States are chassis speed v and independent left/right wheel angular speeds. First advance each wheel by the held motor torque: omegaFree = omega + torque*dt/J. Then solve a strictly convex two-variable box problem: minimize0.5*p^T*A*p-s^T*p, with |p_i|<=mu*N_i*dt. Here s_i=r*omegaFree_i-v, A_ii=r^2/J+1/m and A_ij=1/m. p is an impulse in N s. All nine active-set combinations are enumerated; feasibility and KKT residual are checked. Apply v+=sum(p)/m and omega_i-=r*p_i/J. The equal/opposite rotational reaction changes motor speed, back-EMF, current and the shared bus at the next substep. No extra force is added to DifferentialDrivetrainSim's already-eliminated rolling constraints.

The unconstrained solution enforces zero post-step relative slip (sticking); a bounded impulse permits sliding. Zero torque at rest remains exactly at rest without regularization creep. Encoders receive raw wheel pulse rate and counts, never chassis speed/travel. Thus wheel travel can exceed ground travel under low traction, even while controller readback appears on target. Yaw/lateral/unequal-demand scenarios are explicitly unsupported rather than held silently at zero during a turn.

Mechanical contact loss is p dot s -0.5*p^T*A*p. It consists of p dot postSlip plus a nonnegative projection term. The latter is time-discretization dissipation, including during sticking, NOT measured tire heat. Its decline under timestep refinement is tested. The motor/free-wheel step work uses torque*dt*(omegaOld+omegaFree)/2; a separate mechanical-only work balance checks kinetic energy plus contact loss. This does not repair the empirical motor Kt/Ke discrepancy or certify global electromechanical conservation. Position/angle advance is semi-implicit; finite-step results are compared across launch, slip and sticking at1ms/0.5ms. Numerical bounds are development checks, not physical safety margins.

## Motor source discrepancy is preserved

`references/neo-profile-evidence.json` records exact retrieved-byte hashes and BOTH complete source profiles, with conditions/calibration unresolved. REV's [relative-dynamometer comparison](https://docs.revrobotics.com/brushless/neo/compare) instead reports12V/160A/3Nm/5820rpm/2.065A and explains its comparative purpose. No values are mixed or averaged. A paired short run shows parameter sensitivity, not which profile is true or calibrated. The runner verifies actual instantiated motor constants against each complete evidence profile. No installed-motor/controller test conditions are inferred.
