# Finite CORAL upper-exterior contact experiment

This is a constrained, uncalibrated development experiment, not a complete intake or robot. The exact original native compressed and nominal fixture inputs supply sleeve radius, axis, assumed-density inertia, tube dimensions and floor domain. CORAL mass is explicitly the midpoint of the source range, not native proxy volume or a measured specimen.

The hollow tube remains axis-X at Y=0, with an external orientation constraint supplying reaction moment at zero angular velocity. Only its finite upper exterior/top edge and lower floor contact are supported. Bore, general annular end-face, lateral, free rotation, shell bending and material damage are unsupported. The roller axis must remain above the tube top; invalid states fail closed. The native proxy is hollow, but this reduced contact law is not generic 3D shell contact.

A prepositioned tube starts at X=-160 mm, Z=57.15 mm with +0.15 m/s incoming speed. This is not acquisition from rest. A fixed 20 ms offline controller reads independently injected raw encoder counts and an ideal vertical-ray beam at X=100 mm, commands PWM -0.12, and latches zero-duty brake when the beam is blocked. HOLD means beam-triggered stop only, not capture or successful field transfer. Disabled, wrong encoder sign, initially blocked beam and timeout fault handling are explicit; no hardware adapter or deployment is authorized.

Circle-to-finite-upper-segment geometry supplies the normal and tangent. Normal force uses k*overlap*max(0,1+d*overlapRate), with assumed 5000 N/m roller and 50000 N/m floor stiffness, d=5 s/m. Friction is -mu*Fn*tanh(slip/0.01 m/s). Roller mu=0.8 and floor mu=0,0.2,0.4,0.8 are hypotheses, not carpet/PVC measurements. Normal and friction losses, spring potential, sleeve motor work and constrained-body mechanical residual are recorded. Empirical NEO constants do not establish global electromechanical energy conservation, thermal behavior or calibrated current.

All trajectories use the existing ideal shared-supply algebra with unsupported regeneration rejected, not clamped or silently repaired. The nominal no-initial-interference geometry is a separate native configuration, not a guarantee that gravity cannot create subsequent contact. Refined 0.1/0.05 ms trajectories compare six kinematic and five electrical/loss/residual components at aligned 20 ms samples. Numerical tolerances are development checks, not manufacturing or safety limits. Unsupported runs remain outcomes rather than successful feed claims.

The runner executes fresh native extraction, strict native/Python/Java test counts, and pins source, graph context, consumed native properties, input, compiled classes and retained reports. Execution flags remain false until every success gate. Reproducibility and exact-byte consistency do not authenticate materials or validate the physical model.

Scoring, capture, release, powerless recovery (R204), piece-damage safety (R206), actual REV firmware, full assembly mass, heat, weight distribution and full robot acceptance remain unverified. All required engineering acceptance domains remain blocked. No team strategy is selected.
