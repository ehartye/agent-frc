# Retained lateral pregrip registration

The previous moving FUEL closure miss is preserved. This is a different architecture: hold the piece at the original catcher station X=400 mm, close receiving wheels around it, verify grip, then release. No existing trajectory is silently moved to a new station.

## Native component and guide work

The exact previously pinned 4-inch NOTE, 2-inch CORAL and 3-inch FUEL compliant-wheel STEP files were downloaded again, hash-checked and imported. Bare wheels are rotated to vertical shafts and placed laterally around the original nominal floor-resting body. This keeps the floor intact, unlike the earlier 400 mm-span top/bottom roller-bank envelope, which intersects the original floor in all three seasons.

Fresh native checks establish only these bounded facts:
- All six closed bare-wheel placements have rigid body interference under the assumed 1 mm compression placement. This is not a validated normal force.
- Every wheel also intersects the old side-pad volume. The pads must be replaced, not left underneath the new wheels. Earlier side-pad retention/control claims therefore do not automatically carry over.
- Bare wheels clear the floor by more than 13 mm for NOTE, 50 mm for CORAL and 62 mm for FUEL at nominal placement.
- A conservative box enclosing each entire 60 mm lateral stroke, enlarged by 2 mm total translation error and 2 mm required clearance, clears the original floor, rear gate and bumper.
- The original 32 mm guide aperture passes that box screen for CORAL but cannot certify NOTE/FUEL. A proposed 40 mm aperture passes for all three. An inflated-box overlap is not proof that the actual wheel collides: nominal closed NOTE wheel/guide distance is about 4.09 mm. The larger aperture is a conservative design candidate, not a manufacturing or structural approval.

## Linkage envelope and remaining hardware

The wider NOTE lateral opening needs a different nominal belt span. The pinned supplier snapshot identifies TTB-0174, 1500-5M-09, variant 49610234069291; CORAL/FUEL retain TTB-0164, 1000-5M-09. The same provisional clamp/end exclusions leave about 22.4/43.45/12.9 mm carriage-position margins. These numbers are not installed linkage fit. Pulley and motor extents exceed the reported bare-roller width, and full robot packaging has not been assessed.

Hubs, shafts, bearings, linear guides, belt clamps, offset links and motor mounts are not assembled in this study. Rotating a bare wheel does not validate its supporting shaft or reuse the earlier horizontal wheel-bank stack. No claim is made about current availability or historical season legality of the candidates.

## Control and season integration

A retained pregrip sequence needs a fresh pose/contact supervisor: maintain body support, close with bounded motion, independently establish contact/load, then release the catcher and command feed. Existing pad evidence is insufficient because those pads are removed. Loss of contact or closing failure must not authorize release.

The lateral wheel arrangement initially feeds horizontally. It cannot be rotated by software into the prior elevated NOTE/FUEL trajectories. Those seasons still need a real orientation/launcher transition; CORAL still needs an installed trough transform and placement/exclusion evidence. Sourced mass bounds, low-friction rollback cases and moving-FUEL no-contact controls remain unchanged. All scoring and physical acceptance remain unknown.
