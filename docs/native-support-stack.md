# Provisional supported FUEL-bank layout

`python -B scripts/inspect-support-stack.py` replays a hash-pinned native diagnostic, not a full robot simulation or fabrication release. It uses temporary manufacturer hub, socket screw, bearing and collar CAD plus original analytic shaft/spacer envelopes. See the numeric receipt in `case-studies/2026/native-support-stack.json` and prior rejected accessories in `native-accessory-stack.md`.

The original proposed six wheel centers are +/-17.4625, +/-52.3875 and +/-87.3125mm. Bearing centers are +/-125mm on a hypothetical300mm shaft. Inter-wheel spacer widths are6.35mm; end spacers are19.43125mm. All spacers are original17mmOD/14mmID annuli with no selected stock, supplier SKU or material. A positive-end narrow collar center at132.14375mm places its outer envelope at135.31875mm, leaving14.68125mm to the hypothetical shaft end. No driven pulley/coupling is allocated that allowance.

The native diagnostic found zero common volume for the tested adjacent opposed socket screws, each screw against the inter-wheel spacer, and the positive-end spacer against hub, socket screw and bearing. Bearing/collar common volume was also zero. These are pairwise rigid geometry checks; they do not prove proper face contact, bearing-race load paths, assembly-tool access or axial preload.

The original shaft envelope extrudes a12.7mm-across-flats hex clipped by a13.75mm corner circle, matching the nominal external profile described in the [REV shaft guide](https://docs.revrobotics.com/ion-build/motion/shafts-spacers-collars). It has no center hole, end threads, tolerances, material or physical inertia. A30-degree clocking relative to the initial construction was necessary to avoid interference with native hub/collar bores. At this phase, tested shaft/hub, shaft/collar, shaft/bearing and shaft/end-spacer common volumes were zero. A deliberately misclocked shaft/hub control overlaps approximately67.08mm3. Do not replace a rounded-hex positive drive with a round shaft based on bore diameter alone.

Pinned supports originate from the [bearing product page](https://www.revrobotics.com/ION-Bearings/) and [collar product page](https://www.revrobotics.com/Shaft-Collars-0.5in-Hex-Bore/): REV-21-1916 and REV-21-2900. Source hashes and actual native bounds are in the receipt. The native collar model includes its screw geometry, but no permissible torque or holding load was inferred.

Still missing: actual wheels in this support diagnostic, opposite-end native replay, complete shaft fastener/drive interface, housing/retainer/native mounting screws, toleranced end float, actual engagement, loads and calibration. Previous wheel/hub checks are separate evidence, not an automatically accepted combined assembly. Positive and negative controls here establish diagnostic behavior only. All engineering and procurement acceptance stays blocked. This is a useful nominal geometric starting point for an explicitly uncalibrated simulation fixture, not a simulation-ready calibrated season prototype.
