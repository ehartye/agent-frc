# Check the actual object after the holder opens

An empty mechanism can reach every commanded pose while a released piece strikes the fixed frame below it. Follow one finite physical piece from its admitted position through approach, release, departure and its intended receiving region. Compare a credible jam and the complete recovery to a known ready state.

Run `python scripts/loaded_transfer.py PROJECT loaded-study.json`. The study schema is `agent-frc/loaded-transfer@1`. Supply the project-owned current STEP, an `id` and duty `basis`, gravity in mm/s², a trace tolerance in mm and `piece:{id,file,solid}` with the complete piece's pinned STEP. Each case has a unique `id`, `duty` (delivery or jam-reset), a measured `admissionGapMm` maximum against the physical holder, a frozen two-element `windowS`, the expected `terminalBoundsMm` in the CAD frame, and a pinned `trace`. Bounds use [minimum X,Y,Z, maximum X,Y,Z].

The actual trace identifies the same case, STEP and piece SHA-256, physical `pieceId`, installed `holder` solid and source. Each frame has its executed `timeS`, `custody` and `offsetsMm` mapping the physical piece and each translated moving member to its millimetre displacement from registered geometry. Keep the full installed assembly as a collision obstacle. List adjacent finite pieces or fixtures in `stock` with separate physical IDs, actual pinned `file`, `solid`, `offsetMm` and `basis`. The original trace gives the same `stockIds`; every registered remaining item stays in the checked geometry. Held and recovery translations are linear within an observed interval. A `free` interval starts from rest in the downward Z direction and follows the declared gravitational acceleration. The solver checks the later positions against that motion and computes a conservative bound on intermediate travel. A released, contact-guided trace uses `guided`, a named installed `guide`, pinned original `trajectorySource` and `interpolationBasis`. Use a finer recorded path or a supported trajectory solver where linear interpolation cannot describe the actual motion. For a rotating article, provide each observed rigid pose, angular velocity and inertia basis. The reviewer sweeps the full rotating shape between frames and checks its complete occupied volume during the terminal dwell. [Finite rotating articles](operating-intent.md#finite-rotating-articles)

A successful delivery ends with `received` and the entire named object inside the registered receiving region. A recovery includes `jammed`, ends with `recovery`, identifies the measured rearm using `resetObserved:true` and `readyOffsetsMm` for every moving installed member, and must return the object to the registered region within the declared clock. Explicitly register qualified intentional interfaces by segment index in `interfaces`. A passed opening, empty return, or a different object reaching a station cannot close this duty. Preserve one fixed bridge crossing the released object’s route plus a corrected matched case. An intentionally blocked jam retains its independent consequence and recovery evidence.

Include the relevant physical handoff IDs in the independent reopen record’s `transferMissions` and map each ID to its pinned study in the readiness issue’s `transferClaims`. A project with no transfer in scope uses an empty list and map. Review scope against the selected robot role before approving these declarations. This process checks mechanical clearance and delivery in the stated model. Match scoring requires separate rule- and actor-bound evidence.

## Reconcile the observed run

Inspect `corridorStatus`, `executionStatus` and `executionReason` for each case. The initial pose checks the whole object against all installed geometry and adjacent stock, including its holder. After introducing an article to a native fixture, reapply the commanded joint pose and read back actual positions and bounds before collecting evidence.

For a complete duty, give the case an `execution` file pin to the independently recorded `agent-frc/observed-transfer@1` simulation or instrument output. It retains the original case, duty, source STEP and piece digests, actual piece and holder IDs, stock, entire clock, custody and placed translations. The raw `frames` must match the proposed replay exactly. Record the article's mass, source-frame center of mass, terminal outcome and one observation per frame. Each supported sample includes `accelerationMmS2` and contacts with an installed body, world `pointMm` and signed `forceOnPieceN`. The geometry must meet at that point. The checker reconciles translation and moment equilibrium under the fixed-orientation or constant principal-axis spin model. Delivery identifies the receiver and its accepted piece. Recovery records the obstructed actuator's lost commanded travel and the final observed ready state. A guide that loses contact or requires an unverified sliding contact cannot close an executed duty.

The operator who observed the simulation or physical recording keeps an `agent-frc/observed-transfer-inventory@1` file in the project. Pin it on the transfer study as `executionInventory`, with its `file` and `sha256`. A study written before that field existed names the file through `FRC_TRANSFER_EXECUTIONS` instead. Its `observed` object maps the original log SHA-256 to `{origin,observer,producer,basis}`. Use `executed-simulation` or `physical-measurement` as the origin. Inspect and retain the underlying run before recording its digest. An execution the inventory does not list leaves the completion claim open. The installed analytical shutter exercise demonstrates delivery and a loaded jam with a balanced finite article. The result describes the modeled or measured conditions recorded by its observer.

Run `python examples/mechanical-readiness/observed-shutters.py NEW_EXERCISE`. The exercise writes its inventory to `NEW_EXERCISE/observations.json`. Point `FRC_TRANSFER_EXECUTIONS` at that file, then run `python scripts/loaded_transfer.py NEW_EXERCISE/candidate study.json`. A supported sample supplies the next world contact point when it moves on a face. The checker follows that point across the trimmed article and support surfaces and checks the reaction moments at both ends. The exercise creator writes both the candidate and its observations.

When the study qualifies installed hardware, each frame may supply `nativeCoordinates` keyed by calibrated joint group. Use Fusion native units (centimeters for slides, radians for rotation). An omitted coordinate stays at its verified saved value. The reviewer reconstructs every installed member throughout the actual linear time segments from the native saved pose and isolated prismatic calibrations. Independent admitted cargo and stock retain their own placements. A stationary machine cannot move its holder because an external simulator assigned it a translation. A rotating installed mechanism in a loaded transfer needs an orientation-aware solver. The standalone fixture result explicitly records the scope of its independent motion model.

## Register the executed trajectory

Set `trajectorySchema` on the independently commissioned mission to `agent-frc/executed-trajectory@1`. In the original execution supply a `trajectory` with that schema, a `segments` array, the complete `series` of synchronized observation file pins, and `errorMm: 0` and `errorRad: 0` for an exact numerical law. Nonzero or unknown motion error needs an independently bounded swept-error model. Use the SHA-256 of the canonical trajectory object for `trajectorySha256` in both the proposed trace and the external execution inventory. A guided trace uses the schema identifier as its `interpolationBasis` and pins its original execution as `trajectorySource`.

Every segment names its adjacent primary timestamps in `windowS`. Use `constant-velocity` for supported linear center motion and `gravity-from-rest` for a contiguous free descent. An angular segment uses `constant-orientation` or the measured `constant-world-angular-velocity` law. Installed axes use `linear-native-coordinates` and retain the source-verified moving members and link ratios. Numerical simulation output with a varying acceleration, external perturbing force, missing error bound or unknown interpolation cannot establish this duty through a chord between plotted endpoints.

Each additional source series uses `agent-frc/trajectory-observations@1`. It identifies the same case, article and STEP and article digests. Supply its exact `windowS` and every time-ordered measurement in `samples`. Each row has `timeS` and `centerMm` or `articlePose`. Supported additional channels include `velocityMmS`, `accelerationMmS2`, `angularVelocityRadS`, `nativeCoordinates`, `endBoundsMm`, `donorGapMm`, `normalReactionsN` and `externalForceN` (zero for this supported model). The reviewer compares the recorded channels against the bounded law and observed support on that same clock, including samples between the primary frames. Unsupported physical channels leave the duty unqualified.

An independent reviewer can close this duty when the commissioned native source, recipient and finite terminal region agree with the externally registered execution, all governing laws and measurements reconcile, the complete bounded sweep meets clearance and dwell, and the required physical delivery and jam/reset controls pass. Run the ordinary review and a matched adverse control that actually leaves the region. These checks apply to the named duty and the stated model.

## Verify an admission decision on its own clock

A project may require that a specific article remain accepted throughout the final dwell of a delivery. Put a `terminalObservations` map on the independently selected `transferCriteria` mission. For each applicable delivery case specify the named `channel`, integer tick period `periodNs` and tick-zero time `originNs` on the execution clock. For example, a synthetic inspection can require `{"deliver":{"channel":"both seat switches active","periodNs":10000000,"originNs":0}}`. Freeze the criterion before evaluating the candidate. Choose a dwell whose start and end lie on the observation clock.

In the execution inventory, add `terminalObservation` to the entry for the exact execution digest. Its `record` and `source` are separate project-relative file pins (`file` and `sha256`). The source is the original acquisition. The reviewer independently registers the extraction and confirms how its channel identified the finite article. Write the record as `agent-frc/terminal-observation@1` with `executionSha256`, `missionSha256`, `sourceSha256`, `pieceId`, `case`, the mission's full `recipient` object and the exact `channel`. Compute the mission digest with `transfer_mission.identity(mission)`.

Each ordered sample includes its original integer `tick`, integer `timeNs` and `acceptedId`. Use the observed ID for acceptance, or JSON `null` for an observed denial. Leave unobserved ticks absent. With the example clock, tick 101 must have timestamp 1010000000 ns. The commissioned check requires every tick including both endpoints. A missing tick leaves the result open. An observed denial or another ID fails, even if another tick is missing. Retain the original cadence when exporting a chart or a reduced kinematic trace.

Run the commissioned duty through `mechanical_readiness.py` with the project’s external operating plan and execution inventory. The result establishes the recorded decision at the sampled times. A switch closure, optical estimate or solver contact flag has the physical meaning evidenced by its own source. Continue to verify measured load, contact location, geometry and dynamics through the normal transfer review. The standalone `loaded_transfer.py` run can exercise the geometric study; a registered terminal decision needs its commissioned mission.

## Check momentum at each transition

Compare incoming and outgoing article velocity at every clock boundary, even when ownership changes. The outgoing velocity from a gravity interval includes the acceleration over its full duration. The required impulse is particle mass in kilograms times the change of velocity in metres per second. A supported constant-speed segment and a custody label supply no instantaneous horizontal impulse. The reviewer also checks angular velocity on both sides in the same physical orientation. A change in spin requires separate impact dynamics.

A supported impact case is the centered arrest of a falling article by a stationary frictionless receiver with zero normal restitution. The observer identifies the installed receiver, exact time and physical point, surface normal, signed impulses on article and receiver, and the qualifying contact-model or measurement basis. Pin the original `agent-frc/contact-impulse@1` through `trajectory.impulses` and register its digests in the external observer's `impulseSources`. The reviewer checks the contacted faces, incoming approach, outgoing normal speed, tangential continuity, center-of-mass line of action and both impulse vectors. The shutter exercise supplies a runnable positive capture. Remove the impact source or reverse its direction to reproduce an unqualified capture. An off-center impact, frictional grip, changing spin or driven collision requires a separately qualified model.

When the robot travels between handling stations, compare the loaded robot footprint against the five other field robots on the same phase clock. Preserve the physical piece IDs and the instant an occupied route first prevents a later handoff. [Loaded ground route](loaded-route.md)
