# Individual MAXLift roller tracks

The exact block and standard tube CAD revisions from the corner study now have separate roller-surface witnesses. Eight actual trimmed half-cylinder faces are selected by radius and axis direction, grouped into four rollers by native axis location. These are geometry invariants for the pinned revision, not face indices.

With tube rotation −90° about X and translation (31.745, 300, 0) mm, witnesses lie at (19.045, −0.005, ±22.8) mm for the small rollers and (22.05, 0, ±25.4) mm for the large rollers. Each point lies on an actual trimmed cylinder and the actual tube surface. Native edge/tube intersections retain all 60 mm of each longitudinal contact track, covering tube Y translation 300→360 mm. These are nominal point tracks, not full contact patches, preload, tolerance or load qualification.

Hole controls matter: a centerline track on the tube's Z=25.4 face retains zero length because of the scribe/holes; a track offset 1 mm retains about 37.087 mm; a 3 mm offset retains 60 mm. The native check does not silently replace perforated tube with an infinite plane.

Whole-pair minimum distance is insufficient. Moving the rail +0.005 mm in X leaves that minimum at zero while both small-roller contacts open by 0.005 mm. Moving +5 mm still leaves the large rollers touching while the small pair opens 5 mm. At +10 mm all four contacts are lost. One block therefore does not establish captive restraint. An opposing block, fastening, longitudinal constraint, stops, the support-web attachment and loaded behavior remain separate work.

No catcher forces, hardware safety, installed season transforms or scoring are inferred. Existing moving-FUEL no-contact and old-pad invalidation controls remain unchanged.
