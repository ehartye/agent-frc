# Reconcile the operating roster before reading the parked pose

Select the operating duty from the project's commissioning plan before inspecting the candidate. Pin the plan in the study as `operatingRequirements` with its `file`, `sha256` and JSON `pointer`. The reviewer holds the approved plan outside the candidate tree and supplies its path in `FRC_OPERATING_REQUIREMENTS`. The default selected section is `/operatingIntent`. Set `FRC_OPERATING_PROFILE` when the approved operating section lives elsewhere. Both the section and the file bytes must agree at private issuance.

The selected section contains the canonical `authorityId`, `configuration`, `mode` (fixed or articulated), its engineering `basis`, each required group's `motionRanges` in mm or degrees, and `nativeCoordinates` keyed by those group names. Each coordinate identifies the joint's `type`, rooted `occurrence`, `name` and `coordinate`. Include the scoped `transferMissions` and `externalIssues` even if the approved lists are empty. Capture the requirements of purchased equipment in the same commissioning plan. A supplier's rigid STEP representation can support a fixed installation review when the duty permits. A duty requiring its internal travel needs a source model and qualified poses representing that motion.

The station collects the occurrences, owning component counts, joints, rigid groups, suppressed states, solver health and motion links when the selected archive reopens. Hidden parts remain in scope. Every installed occurrence needs a native constraint path to the declared anchor. A mounted contact and a loose removable piece can have identical saved geometry. Test a truly fixed assembly with a real rigid constraint and test removal under its own operating duty. For sliders and revolute axes connected by motion links, measure the drive and its followers under the installed coupling. Other unresolved constraints stay open. Keep access, retention and contact qualifications scoped to the actual loads and positions.

[Freeze the required physical articles](article-functions.md) before opening an earlier assembly. Pin their roster in the same commissioning plan.

## Commission a mechanically coupled drive

Freeze each group's operating interval in millimeters or degrees. The loaded review applies that interval to every achieved state, including both endpoints, throughout delivery and recovery. A separate unloaded service calibration may travel farther. Check the complete piece inside the receiver within the permitted stroke.

For a linked carriage, opposing sliders or a rack and pinion, register the physical joint identity of every driver and follower in `nativeCoordinates`. Use a driver-only Fusion request with those same coordinates and a list of named `frames`. Each frame has an `id` and a `commands` map of independent native coordinates in centimeters or radians. Include a short identifiable calibration for each axis, extremes, combined positions and a return to the saved position. Drive the actual coupled assembly in Fusion using `fusion_link_calibration.capture(app, project, lineage_file, request, fresh_output, station_key)`. The station reads the live followers, link ratios, reversals and full occurrence transforms. Independent revolute joints use the same capture with an empty motion-link inventory.

Name the fixed anchor and each moving tip in `landmarks`. Identify its original native solid using `occurrence`, `body` and the exact local vertex in `localMm`. Register `landmarkTrials` by group and frame, with the tip, stationary anchor, independently expected signed displacement vector `deltaMm` and measurement `toleranceMm`. Choose a physical tooth, arm tip or flange vertex off the rotation axis. The station measures the same assembly-context vertex again after the driver command. Compare both the moving tip and fixed anchor with their source placements before counting a command as an operating pose.

In the independent commissioning plan, identify each follower under `motionLinks` with its `driver` and signed `nativeRatio` (follower radians or centimeters per driver native unit). Supply the approved saved 4×4 world transforms of the associated moving and fixed joint members in `linkedReferencePoses`. Include a keyed or clocked constituent when its installed phase matters. List the witnessed operating sequences as arrays of named poses in `coupledOperatingPaths`. The normal readiness study pins the station record as `linkCalibration`. Use those pose identifiers for the mandatory contact census. The reviewer derives travel about the actual axes, compares every installed member across measured states and sweeps the resulting full STEP move between them. An unresolved intermediate clearance keeps the decision open.

For a measured load and jam replay, each frame reports all attained native coordinates and the offsets of installed occurrence origins. A component with a revolving ancestor also records its 4×4 world displacement from rest in `installedTransforms`. A captured range may span multiple turns. The follower must maintain signed phase or stop with the blocked mechanism. Test the finite contact interfaces during a unilateral obstruction, then verify the reset on the same clock. The full transfer review tracks the orientation of the finite article. Rotating gear teeth elsewhere in the assembly are included in the geometric sweep. An unmeasured slip or turning support needs an independently bounded contact model.

For an isolated diagnostic, `python scripts/linked_motion.py PROJECT study.json` authenticates the native station and returns attained coordinates for the exact selected occurrence paths. Pin `authority`, `lineage`, `archive`, `step`, `jointAuthority`, `linkCalibration` and the independently held `commissioning` JSON. Identify the `anchor` and `installedOccurrences`. Set `FRC_NATIVE_WITNESS_PUBLIC_KEY` and `FRC_OPERATING_REQUIREMENTS` to their reviewer-held copies. An optional pinned `obstruction` study identifies the exact STEP, named parts with their complete STEP occurrence paths and native occurrences, the pairs to measure, and finite joint coordinates for both a nominal and a locked or displaced case. The report gives each pair's actual common volume and gap. It distinguishes a hypothetical frozen part from observed damage, actuator protection or successful recovery.


## Commission the physical destination

For each entry in `transferMissions`, add its `transferCriteria` record to the independently selected operating plan. Identify the exact archive and STEP, finite article ID, source solid and file digest. For both donor and recipient record the rooted Fusion occurrence, native body name and matching STEP solid. Describe a `handoff` between distinct supports or a `retained-inspection` on the same support. Freeze the full terminal box, its frame, allowed position error, dwell in seconds, minimum fraction of article weight borne by the recipient, required donor clearance, and case IDs, duties and clock windows. The keyed [teaching exercise](../examples/mechanical-readiness/coupled_inspection.py) is an executable example.

Use `saved-root` for assembly coordinates. A `recipient-in-saved-root` box follows a translating recipient from its saved location. A changing frame orientation requires a separately qualified model. Register each actual solver execution against the SHA-256 of the canonical mission object in the independent observer inventory. The verifier checks observed forces, identity and complete article bounds through the commissioned terminal interval. For a handoff it also checks sustained physical separation from the donor. Use a zero dwell only for a deliberately instantaneous inspection.

For a free departure, record the finite article and donor just before separation, at the first geometrically clear observation and throughout the subsequent motion. Verify the matched frozen and reversed actuator controls. The [release-onset checker](../scripts/release_onset.py) checks observed rigid poses, donor reaction and finite separation over bounded interpolated translation and rotation. Record the underlying trajectory approximation error for both bodies. Register each source-bound result's study in `releaseOnsets` on the case and each digest in its independent observation. This pair check contributes to the complete installed traversal.

Register known Boolean discrepancies under `numericalInterfaces` in the independent commissioning plan, or use an empty object after reviewing the interface inventory. Each named case pins the native issue, its STEP, the installed solid pair, approved pair allowance, numerical tolerance, interior witness points, boundary reserve and complementary cylindrical partitions. The [interface checker](../scripts/interface_consistency.py) measures disjoint material inside both parts and compares a complete cylindrical region with its core and ring. Zero on a threaded common-volume query cannot override a positive enclosed volume or an unresolved partition. The standalone placed-source check can replay a supplier interface before incorporation into the full assembly.

### Bound an implausibly large intersection

If a common-volume query puts nearly an entire fastener inside its mate, add `excludedPointsMm` to the same commissioned case. Key each list of installed XYZ points by the exact solid occurrence containing those points. Pick centers visibly inside that solid and outside its mate, including any internal voids. Keep separate `interiorPointsMm` in the shared material. Run `python scripts/interface_consistency.py PROJECT STUDY.json` or rerun the ordinary readiness review with the case in `numericalInterfaces`.

The checker classifies centers against both solids, measures distance to every shell and subtracts the registered boundary reserve. Within each member it trims intersecting witness balls before summing their volumes. Subtracting that excluded volume from the integrated member volume gives a ceiling for the intersection. If both members have witnesses, the tighter ceiling applies. The receipt retains the estimated integration error, the raw Boolean and both bounds. A center on a boundary supplies no ball. A registered member with no usable excluded witness keeps the numerical check open.

The ceiling inherits the estimated numerical integration error of the source volume. Compare the raw result with the interval using the commissioned numerical tolerance. A borderline margin requires an independent measurement. A shared lower bound above the allowed overlap proves a failure even when the reported amount is impossible. An interval containing the raw result may still be too wide to distinguish competing measurements. Qualifying a seat, a spatial fit, retention or load requires its own evidence.

Under `structuralRoutes`, identify the loaded body, the true chassis anchor and the structural members eligible for each scoped load case. Test the direction and design load of each interface. A skin, cover or internally connected fastener group cannot complete the route. The native [path screen](../scripts/structural_path.py) can rule out a proposed route when even the solid bounds are separated. The complete mechanical review separately checks retention, directional bearing area and allowable pressure or installed joint qualification. Register every simultaneous point force (N and mm), free couple (N·mm) and gravity vector (mm/s²), including explicit zeroes. The reviewer uses the cold body masses and placed solid centroids for uniform bodies. It resolves three forces and three moments per member, with action and reaction at the same installed location. Every compressive reaction must have a feasible pressure on the finite shared face within its qualified allowable. A single net force can hide an overturning or twisting couple. Read the residuals, bearing bounds and required pressure center in the result. Fixed locations and linearly varying forces can be qualified between observations through convex interpolation of the endpoint reactions. Moving structural geometry, changing load locations and unresolved pressure distributions stay open. Fatigue remains a separate duty.

For `mountDatums`, use local drawing or original supplier landmarks and the target world coordinates from the assembly definition. Register at least three noncollinear physical points per nominated occurrence, its tolerance and engineering basis. The reviewer checks the actual signed Fusion placement and the corresponding solid surfaces. A wholly fixed issue requires coverage of every placed occurrence. In an articulated robot, commission every installed purchased occurrence, including its nested physical leaf and the repeated instances of a supplier assembly. Compare corresponding original solid features through the full occurrence transform to the intended site. An outer wrapper can be correctly located while its child cancels the transform. Matching a generated STEP cannot establish correct installation when the native design was already assembled in the wrong place.

The [mount datum screen](../scripts/mount_datums.py) can reject an installation from an earlier partial native observation. It checks a measured assembly-context vertex against the original component coordinate, its saved transform and the independent target. A passing single landmark leaves mounting qualification open. For external observers, each site may instead identify its exact physical leaf through `nativePathPointer` together with the independently checked `proxyErrorMmPointer`, `localMmPointer` and measured `worldMmPointer`.

## Test a compression-only seat

A project with an existing measured support study can run `python scripts/structural_path.py PROJECT study.json` with `agent-frc/observed-supports@1`, pinned `archive`, `step` and `observation`. For each case, supply the common compressive reaction `normal`, JSON pointers for simultaneous applied points and forces, free couples and each measured face patch’s centroid and area. Express all directions in one frame and retain the source time for each simultaneous load. The report names forces that would require the seat to pull, provide shear or resist a twisting couple. A pure moment with zero resultant load also requires a different support.

Add a `bounds` pointer to each patch when the observation includes its enclosing root-frame `[xmin,ymin,zmin,xmax,ymax,zmax]` in mm. With complete bounds for coplanar seats, an outlying pressure center can prove the load tips beyond the physical support. A center inside the enclosing box can still fall outside the actual faces. A failed centroid-only fit stays open unless a necessary condition is violated. Compare the correct frame, a reversed load, and the same load with a measured corrective reaction before choosing a design. Name and qualify the hold-down, shear interface or other additional load path in the full readiness review. The observed-seat screen always leaves installed capacity open.

## Finite rotating articles

Record the actual 4×4 root-frame rigid displacement in `articlePose` at every solver frame and corresponding observation. Record the center of mass in the source solid, the achieved angular velocity in rad/s, and a source-bound inertia tensor in kg·mm² or an explicit uniform mass distribution. A fixed-orientation study records `orientationModel: fixed-in-saved-axes`. The reviewer carries the entire solid through the bounded segments, including intermediate angles and recipient dwell. The supported angular model is a constant principal-axis spin with measured, balanced contact forces and a verified path on the finite bearing surfaces. An accelerating spin, uncertain contact, or ambiguous full turn requires a more complete execution. A loose key rotated clear of one shaft is an interference diagnostic. Commission its engagement with both keyways, relative phase, axial retention and torque path before qualifying the drive.

## Check torque through a straight key

Can the shaft deliver the required torque to the hub after the key moves axially? Test the two flanks that carry load in the selected direction, and include the radial support that reacts the resulting force. A key can clear the motor and still have too little contact with the hub.

Run the [three-position teaching exercise](../examples/mechanical-readiness/keyed-torque.py) from the plugin root with its CAD Python environment:

```console
python examples/mechanical-readiness/keyed-torque.py
```

The script builds and reopens three original STEP fixtures. Their flat sections represent the shaft, key, driven hub and radial bearing surface. Each replay applies the same 150 N·mm moment about the shaft axis. For this exercise the allowable contact pressure is 2 MPa on the two flanks and 10 MPa on the radial support. The structural route evaluator measures the common faces and solves for force and moment balance on each member.

| Key position | Measured hub flank area | Result for this load |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Seated | 24 mm² | **Pass.** The reactions balance the applied moment. |
| Withdrawn 8 mm | 8 mm² | **Open.** The evaluator leaves about 60.35 N·mm unbalanced on the hub. |
| Withdrawn 14 mm | 0 mm² | **Fail.** The key has left the hub and the required contact is missing. |

For a real drive, use the drawing and measured installation to establish key length, axial stop locations, backlash and loaded phase. Check the actual flanks after taking up backlash in each commissioned torque direction. Test both axial travel limits, the hub’s operating support and the torque demanded by the mechanism. Attach the capacity evidence for each interface and qualify key shear, bearing pressure, axial retention and fatigue at the required duty. The fixture’s hypothetical pressure limits apply only to the exercise.

Record the members, directed interfaces, pressure evidence and load cases under `structuralRoutes` in the independently approved operating plan. Pin that plan through `operatingRequirements` as described above. Private issuance compares the candidate with the reviewer’s plan and runs the required routes on the selected native issue and its placed STEP. Omitting a commissioned flank or leaving a required route open prevents approval.
