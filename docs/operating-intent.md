# Reconcile the operating roster before reading the parked pose

Select the operating duty from the project's commissioning plan before inspecting the candidate. Pin the plan in the study as `operatingRequirements` with its `file`, `sha256` and JSON `pointer`. The reviewer holds the approved plan outside the candidate tree and supplies its path in `FRC_OPERATING_REQUIREMENTS`. The default selected section is `/operatingIntent`. Set `FRC_OPERATING_PROFILE` when the approved operating section lives elsewhere. Both the section and the file bytes must agree at private issuance.

The selected section contains the canonical `authorityId`, `configuration`, `mode` (fixed or articulated), its engineering `basis`, each required group's `motionRanges` in mm or degrees, and `nativeCoordinates` keyed by those group names. Each coordinate identifies the joint's `type`, rooted `occurrence`, `name` and `coordinate`. Include the scoped `transferMissions` and `externalIssues` even if the approved lists are empty. Capture the requirements of purchased equipment in the same commissioning plan. A supplier's rigid STEP representation can support a fixed installation review when the duty permits. A duty requiring its internal travel needs a source model and qualified poses representing that motion.

The station collects the occurrences, owning component counts, joints, rigid groups, suppressed states, solver health and motion links when the selected archive reopens. Hidden parts remain in scope. Every installed occurrence needs a native constraint path to the declared anchor. A mounted contact and a loose removable piece can have identical saved geometry. Test a truly fixed assembly with a real rigid constraint and test removal under its own operating duty. An active dependent motion link or an unsupported multi-axis constraint remains open until its complete dependent motion is calibrated. Keep access, retention and contact qualifications scoped to the actual loads and positions.
