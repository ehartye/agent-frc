# Compare the contact shape used by a simulator with installed CAD

Before interpreting a handling or drive result, identify the physical article and the collision shape that applied force in the solver. Compare their mounting position and occupied surface independently. Two smooth opposed rollers can keep exactly the same opening after their axes move apart and their radii grow. That substitution changes where the piece first meets a wheel and its surface speed at the same shaft speed.

Run `python scripts/collider_comparison.py PROJECT STUDY.json` from the installed package. The study has schema `agent-frc/collider-comparison@1` and two independent file/digest pins, `reference` and `simulation`. The result covers one issued STEP occurrence and the registered poses and points. Run other members separately. Keep proposed stations, undeveloped tread growth and loaded deformation in independent design or operating cases. [Moving mass and inertia](articulated-mass.md) · [Loaded motion and custody](loaded-transfer.md)

## Register the CAD evidence

Supply the canonical `lineage` pin, `solid` key, full placed `identity`, and `archiveSha256`, `stepSha256` and `configuration` from the Fusion authority. Name the common `frame`, simulator `geom`, and `kind` (`cylinder` or `box`). Pin the original CAD datum and pose observations in `sources`. Supply the mount's row-major, local-to-project 4×4 transform in `savedMountToFrameMm`. Its origin represents the primitive center; its Z axis is the cylinder axis. A box uses its three half-size axes. All translations in the reference are millimetres. Supply independently observed transforms for every registered pose in the `poses` map. Using an analytic mapping at interpolated positions requires independent evidence for that mapping.

Choose `surfacePointsLocalMm` as a map from witness names to actual XYZ points in the saved mount frame. Include opposing finite working surfaces and useful points on both axial ends. A witness inside a rim opening cannot stand in for the trim. Choose the project's `tolerance` limits for `mountMm`, `extentMm`, `surfaceMm` and `axisLineDeg` before examining the candidate. The method reopens the selected STEP, checks every point lies on its trimmed boundary, and measures six signed extrema against the mount frame. Preserve all offsets and state the basis for each limit.

## Capture the effective collision geometry

Record the same issue identity, configuration, collider and frame in the independent simulation observation. Pin the actual model under `model` and include it, the effective input files, compilation settings, observer and originating run evidence in `sources`. During a MuJoCo run, after forward kinematics, call `collider_comparison.observe_mujoco(model, data, geom, frame_body)` at each commissioned state. Store its returned row under the corresponding `poses` key. The helper reads the compiled primitive and its pose relative to the named body. Binding the frame body to the CAD datum, the pose/time to the actual run, collision selection, collision margins and physical contact response remain the observer's responsibility. Other solvers can supply equivalent independently captured primitive records.

Each row includes `geom`, `frame`, `kind`, `halfSizesM` (radius and half-length for a cylinder or three half-lengths for a box), and `geomToFrameM` (row-major rigid 4×4, translation in metres). Compare the Euclidean center displacement against the single mount tolerance. Retain its three signed components, signed minimum and maximum extent errors on each CAD axis, and signed distance of each physical witness from the simulator surface. Outside is positive. Cylinder axis tilt measures line alignment; drive direction and transmission require independent evidence.

For an invented 10 mm radius roller, offset a 9 mm proxy by +1 mm. Its +X face stays at +10 mm while the −X witness sits 2 mm outside the proxy. The retained coordinates and separate center residual expose the change. Remove one required pose for an incomplete control. A matching result covers these samples. Concave cutouts, gaps between points, intermediate poses, deformation, traction and scoring require their own evidence. [Simulator selection](simulator-selection.md)
